HandyAI ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: When you create an account, we collect your email address and a unique user ID.
  • Photos and descriptions: Photos and descriptions you submit for AI analysis are sent through our secure server to Anthropic's API for analysis, and are not stored by us beyond your session.
  • Usage data: We track how many AI features you use (stored in Firebase Firestore) to enforce the monthly usage limits on free and Pro plans, along with whether your account currently has an active subscription.
  • Purchase data: Subscription and purchase information is processed by RevenueCat and Google Play. Your account ID is shared with RevenueCat so we can confirm your subscription. We do not store payment card details.
  • App history: Job plans and diagnoses you generate are stored locally on your device and optionally in Firebase Firestore tied to your account.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the HandyAI service
  • To authenticate your account and manage your subscription
  • To enforce free-tier usage limits
  • To send your photos and descriptions to Anthropic's Claude API for AI analysis

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase (Google): Authentication, cloud data storage, and the servers that run HandyAI's AI features — firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Anthropic: AI photo analysis — anthropic.com/privacy
  • RevenueCat: Subscription management — revenuecat.com/privacy
  • Google Play: App distribution and payments — policies.google.com/privacy

4. Where Your Data Is Processed

HandyAI's services run in more than one country, so your information may be processed outside the country where you live:

  • South Africa: your account data, usage records and saved history are stored in Google Cloud's Johannesburg region.
  • United States: when you use an AI feature, your photos and descriptions pass through our servers in the United States and are analysed by Anthropic in the United States. RevenueCat processes your subscription records in the United States.
  • Google's global infrastructure: sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication and payments by Google Play, both operated by Google, which may process this information in other countries.

The data protection laws of these countries may differ from those where you live. We send each service only the information it needs to do its job, all of it is encrypted in transit, and each provider is bound by the privacy commitments linked above. By using HandyAI, you consent to your information being processed in these countries.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account and usage data for as long as your account is active. Photos submitted for analysis are not permanently stored by us. You can request deletion of your account data at any time by contacting us.

6. Children's Privacy

HandyAI is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
