HandyAI – Delete Your Account

Last updated: August 10, 2026

HandyAI, developed by Jason Smith, lets you request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

How to Request Deletion

  • In the app: Open HandyAI, go to Settings, and tap "Delete Account." This opens a pre-filled email to our support address.
  • By email: Send a request to jasonsmithdev.117@gmail.com from the email address registered to your account, with the subject "Account Deletion Request."

What Gets Deleted

Once we process your request, we delete:

  • Your account and login credentials (Firebase Authentication)
  • Your saved diagnosis and job-plan history (Firebase Firestore)
  • Your subscription/customer record with our subscription provider, RevenueCat

What May Be Retained

Google Play retains billing and transaction records for completed purchases independently of HandyAI, per Google's own retention policies. We do not permanently store photos submitted for AI analysis — they are sent to Anthropic's API for processing and are not retained by us.

Processing Time

We process deletion requests within 30 days of receipt and will send you a confirmation email once your account and data have been deleted.

Contact

Questions about this process can be sent to jasonsmithdev.117@gmail.com.
